dcurves

A Python package for decision curve analysis. The method evaluates and compares prediction models and diagnostic tests by their clinical consequences.

79,000+PyPI downloadsPer pepy.tech, 27 Oct 2026

  • Evaluates models for binary and survival outcomes.
  • Calculates net benefit and interventions avoided.
  • Installs from PyPI with pip.
